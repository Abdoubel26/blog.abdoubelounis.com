# Why I Think About Death

I've been thinking about mortality lately...

## The strange thing about death

This book is a masterpiece; if you genuinely apply its principles, your social life will change dramatically. The problem is that you can’t apply all of those principles overnight; the process is gradual and may take months.

## Remembering that I'm going to die
Dale Carnegie was a coach. He would advise his clients to try one of those principles every week, and they would report the results the following week. Most people can't afford a coach, but this doesn’t mean it’s the end. Today, with the existence of AI and LLMs like ChatGPT and Claude, you can use them as your coach. For example, you would use a prompt like this:

You are my personal self-help coach, guiding me through the principles of Dale Carnegie’s teachings, especially those from How to Win Friends and Influence People. Your role is to help me apply each principle one at a time in my daily life.

Each day, assign me one specific principle to focus on. Explain it clearly, give me practical examples, and suggest a small, actionable challenge I can try that day.

Every night, I will return and report what happened, what I tried, what worked, what didn’t, and how I felt. Based on my report, you will:

Reflect on my experience.

Offer feedback

Assign the next principle, or help me retry the current one if needed

Help me build habits, improve my relationships, and grow in confidence and emotional intelligence.

Let’s begin with the first principle and guide me toward lasting personal transformation.

This way, you have access to a free professional coach. If you take it seriously and follow along each day, your social life can transform by 180 degrees. Additionally, you can use this resource for almost anything. For example, I recently aimed to improve my programming skills by learning a new JavaScript framework. I asked ChatGPT to be my coach and help me stay disciplined in pursuing that goal. Each night, I would report on what worked and what didn’t. This method allowed me to track my progress and understand the reasons behind my procrastination or difficulties in learning effectively.
